ClearBill is a free, offline first workbench that teaches you to read a medical bill the way an auditor would.
Every case is fictional, and nothing you type ever leaves the browser.

This emergency room bill in Houston carries seven line items, each priced against a fixed Medicare reference snapshot.
The explanation of benefits reconciles to the cent. Two thousand four hundred fifty three dollars billed, three hundred dollars of patient share.

The audit engine flags four items for review.
Two identical chest X ray charges on the same day, a near duplicate inside the tolerance, an unbundled lab pair, and units above the published cap.
Every flag links back to the exact lines and cites the C M S source behind the check.

The A I reasoning lab turns that audit into a four stage case file. Benchmark, coding, coverage, plan.
A summary strip leads with the questioned range, the protection outcome, and the flag count.

Stage one scores each line's markup against the clinician reference. The hospital facility fee lands twelve times higher, an outlier.
Stage two restates the same findings with a stated confidence and the rule each one rests on.

Stage three walks the coverage tree. This emergency bill routes to a No Surprises Act review, every answer marked yes, no, or unknown.
Stage four orders the appeal, the repeated charges first, then the pair, then assistance.

A what if simulator projects the patient share. Move a slider and the waterfall recomputes on the spot.
With the questioned charges removed and a sixty percent assistance discount, the three hundred dollar share falls to about one hundred.

The appeal plan reads like a checklist, each step with its rationale and questioned amount.
The dispute letter is generated from the same analysis and sealed with an S H A two five six digest.
Print renders it as a single page, ready to send.

ClearBill runs entirely offline once the service worker is ready. No accounts, no tracking, no network calls.
A built in battery reruns twelve self checks on demand, and twelve hundred ninety Node tests verify the engine, the exports, and every edge.
